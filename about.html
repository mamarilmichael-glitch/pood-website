About

Busy sporting family. Stinky gear. Having a crack at fixing it.

Regular detergent was built for regular laundry. It was never built for what happens to a gi that’s been drenched in sweat since 6am, sat in a bag all day, and needs to go back on the mat tomorrow.

POOD started with that exact problem — not a lab, not a marketing brief, but a bag that wouldn’t stop smelling no matter what was thrown at it.

So POOD was built the way a fragrance house builds a scent — properly, with real structure. We're testing samples on our own training gear, not selling a finished product.

Not a detergent with perfume added. A fragrance that happens to do the laundry.

Meet the family

We're a family of six from Brisbane — Mum, Dad and four kids.

Netball. Soccer. BJJ. Scooters. Footy. Dance moves nobody asked for. Four training bags, one washing machine.

That's why we're testing POOD samples on our own gear, after real training. That's the Sweat Test: wash it, train in it, sniff it. We share what we noticed — good or bad.

Where we are right now

POOD is in pre-launch validation. We haven’t purchased inventory, and we haven’t finalised a fragrance — both are being decided through real testing of samples on our own gear, not guesswork.

POOD is currently in pre-launch validation. No product is available for purchase yet.
