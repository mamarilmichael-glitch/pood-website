How It Works

Two jobs. One Pood.

Formulated with an antibacterial agent and enzyme-based cleaning to target odour-causing bacteria in the fabric — with a fragrance layered on top, not instead of it.

The mechanism

Enzyme-Based Cleaning

Breaks down stains and ground-in odour at the fibre level.

Antibacterial Agent

Formulated with a silver-ion antibacterial agent, targeting odour-causing bacteria in the fabric itself.

Laboratory substantiation of finished-formula performance is pending.

What we don't say (and why)

You won't see us say “kills bacteria,” “antimicrobial,” “sanitises,” or any percentage claim like “99.9%.” Those are outcome claims, and outcome claims need laboratory testing and legal clearance we don't have yet.

Ingredients

Sodium lauryl sulfate, Sodium C14-16 olefin sulfonate, Polyvinyl alcohol, Glycerin, Sodium citrate, Perfume, Silver ion (antibacterial agent).
